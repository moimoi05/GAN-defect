STABILITY AND IMAGE REVIEW
The large G gradient norm is concentrated in epoch 1 (69,007); it fell to 13,377 in epoch 2 and 1,646 by epoch 5. D norm also fell. The run did not show a sustained gradient explosion. Loss values oscillated substantially, including G loss from -1066.8 to +1078.2; adversarial losses may change sign, so total losses alone are not an explosion test.

The more serious sign is generator inactivity. Validation mean RGB change fell from 0.1318 at epoch 3 to 0.0243 at epoch 5 (82% decrease), while mask area above 0.1 fell from 0.2813 to 0.0626 (78% decrease). Visual comparison of the fixed validation sample shows a visible generated alteration early, but epochs 4–5 are nearly copies of the normal input with no clear new scratch. This is consistent with suspected identity/no-op collapse. Different outputs retain diversity because their inputs differ; evidence supports a shrinking translation effect rather than classic identical-output collapse. Colored edge artifacts remain in earlier images.

The background-change metric measures RGB difference only outside pixels where the learned alpha mask is >=0.1. It stayed low to moderate (0.0105–0.0362); it does not certify semantic background preservation because the mask itself is learned and becomes sparse.

No automated collapse threshold fired during training; epoch-by-epoch evaluation identified the sharp change in epochs 4–5. The full visual evidence is preserved for review. Early stopping did not trigger: 5 epochs are below warmup 30 and the configured patience is 25.

CONTROLLER CORRECTION
The first controller invocation stopped after epoch 2 because it incorrectly expected exactly 3 G updates per epoch. The implementation correctly carries the remainder of the 5D:1G schedule across epoch boundaries, so the cumulative count after D=36 is G=7. The pilot-only checkpoint at epoch 2 was safe; the corrected controller resumed that pilot checkpoint and completed through epoch 5. It did not load a smoke checkpoint or restart from full-train weights.

RECOMMENDATION
Do not begin full training yet. Resolve or diagnose the shrinking generator effect first, then run a new bounded comparison. There is no evidence supporting gradient clipping: G/D norms became much smaller and remained finite, so clipping could conceal the actual behavior. The LR evidence does not isolate the cause of the identity/no-op drift. Keep 1e-4 as the observed reference and compare a separate short run at G=5e-5, D=1e-4 only as a controlled diagnostic; do not present that as a proven fix. Inspect/check objective balance and scratch-focused image metrics before approving a full run.
