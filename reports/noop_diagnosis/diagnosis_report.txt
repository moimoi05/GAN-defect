DEFECT-GAN F971 NO-OP DIAGNOSIS / 2026-11-06

Conclusion: HOLD full training. Evidence supports alpha-gating collapse driven primarily by an unstable critic/classifier signal, not a disconnected generator or a demonstrated dominance of spatial regularization. Exact causal attribution to optimizer LR/precision/initialization requires a controlled rerun. No new training was performed.



METHOD AND LIMITS

Five original checkpoints; all 9 fixed validation pairs (9 OK, 8 unique NG, NG0 repeated). Native 1024 RGB. Validation inference matches original eval/no-noise BF16. Gradient probes: fixed validation pair 0 only, training mode with stochastic noise, seed 42 reset before each term, BF16 plus activation checkpointing, frozen D parameters, no optimizer. These are local checkpoint gradients, not historical optimizer-group gradients. All shared G parameters counted once. Norms are L2 of gradients of already-weighted losses, without accumulation division; mask/foreground/SPADE subsets overlap only with all. GRAPH / CONDITIONING / LABEL AUDIT

compose_layers = x*(1-M)+M*F. No detach in the G loss path or either reconstruction cycle. d output/dF=M and d output/dM=F-x; the foreground receives attenuated image gradients as M shrinks, while sigmoid derivative M*(1-M) attenuates the mask-logit path. Foreground remains highly different from input even late, so identity is achieved mainly by closing alpha, not by learning F=x.

One shared G and D in the paper variant; optimizer parameter lists deduplicate aliases. Restoration explicitly forces category 0; defacement uses 1. SPADE broadcasts target labels; optional spatial map multiplies category, and does not act as final alpha/ROI. Nonzero SPADE gradient observed in all probes. D outputs raw patch scores and one BCE logit; no sigmoid/softmax on WGAN scores, no degenerate one-class cross-entropy. D real labels NG=1, OK=0; G targets fakeNG=1/fakeOK=0. D step freezes G and detaches fake endpoints; G step freezes D weights but differentiates D with respect to generated image. GP detached endpoints and FP32 input gradient computation are appropriate.

WGAN critic is shared/unconditional: it matches the real OK+NG mixture. At an identity solution the real and fake mixtures coincide, so adversarial loss alone cannot enforce conversion to Scratch. Classification must supply the category-specific signal. This is a limitation of the chosen shared formulation, not an observed missing condition argument in D.

Reference https://arxiv.org/html/2103.15158, Eqs 5-12 and section 4.1. Code uses WGAN-GP substitution, BCE multilabel/single-Scratch adaptation, image L1 cycle reconstruction, paired mask L1 spatial cycle, sum of both mask means spatial constraint. Weights real-cls=2, fake-cls=5, rec=5, spatial-cycle=5, constraint=1; GP=10 is a project convention. The two directions are averaged. No extra target-domain identity term executes in this variant. Symmetric averaging, native 1024, critic pooling/depth and single-class encoding are documented project adaptations; paper is not an exact architectural specification.

M=0 in all four calls gives output=input, rec=0, sd-cycle=0 and sd-constraint=0 regardless of foreground. Thus these losses admit/prefer the no-op subsolution. The weighted-gradient evidence below does NOT establish that their magnitudes dominate: adversarial gradients dominate all five probes, including after collapse. Removing/changing paper losses is unsupported and was not done.

INTERPRETATION AND VISUAL EVIDENCE

Classifier swings both real domains positive at epoch 1 (means 956 and 839), negative at epoch 2, and positive again at epoch 3. This cannot serve as a reliable semantic Scratch score. The first defacement classification alpha gradient is zero for probes 1 and 3 although classification still has a large total gradient from the restoration direction. BCE targets are correct; saturation can remove the corrective signal when a near-input fake already satisfies the current Scratch head. Epoch 4 classification norm is only .042 versus adversarial 2485. This is optimization/semantic-signal failure evidence, not proof of an LR fix.

Visual review covered 31 train NG and 45 validation results. Real defects include narrow dark fissures and larger jagged orange/brown exposed-material scratches/tears, with irregular paths that can cross printed characters or occur on blank material. Generated effects in epochs 1-3 mainly recolor/halo existing letters, holes and material boundaries, with diffuse texture changes. They do not reproduce the reference Scratch morphology. Epochs 4-5 close alpha while foreground still contains saturated high-contrast boundaries; fake becomes close to original. On pair 7 (mostly blank material), boundary modification remains but there is no clear newly synthesized matching jagged Scratch. No pair has a convincing new Scratch in these overview comparisons. This is visual diagnostic judgement, not an annotated detector score; subtle subpixel defects cannot be ruled out by montage.

Every sample-specific numeric record and path is in samples.csv. Fixed edge_delta/nonedge_delta are computed from input local high-pass threshold .025, independent of learned alpha; they are texture-edge proxies, not text segmentation or semantic background masks. Do not interpret them as Scratch precision.



COVERAGE BUG AND MINIMAL FIX

Pilot FullPatchCoverageDataset already covered all 41 clean +31 NG identifiers per epoch. It yields 72 PAIRS, or 144 image exposures with repetitions; it does not mean exactly one exposure of each image. Official config lacked cover_all_manifest_rows_per_epoch and defaulted to 41 pair draws with replacement for NG: expected unique NG ~23, around 8 unseen per epoch. Enabled the existing wrapper in configs/defectgan_1024.yaml; no data, pairing implementation or objective edited. This bug did not cause the audited pilot collapse because the pilot flag was already true.

Shuffle preserves coverage and drop_last=False retains the tail. With accumulation4, 72 pairs give 18 D updates/epoch; global ncritic5 yields cumulative G=3,7,10,14,18. Only eligible groups are used for G updates; full D exposure does not promise every image directly updates G each epoch. That follows the configured 5:1 schedule, not silent dataloader truncation.

Regression: tests/test_noop_audit.py asserts official flag, actual 41/31 unique coverage, 72 pairs, analytic alpha/foreground composition gradients and zero-mask identity. Together with paper baseline and trainer regressions: 39 passed; reports/noop_regression.log. Existing dirty working tree preserved; no commit/push.
